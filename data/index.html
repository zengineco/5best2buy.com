Open datasets

We spent a long time verifying who actually makes independent food in America, one producer at a time. Rather than keep it locked in a website, here it is as data you can download, check and cite. Free for any use, including commercial, as long as you credit it.

Independent US Food Makers1467 records · CSV, JSON · CC BY 4.0 Halal-Certified Independent US Food Makers33 records · CSV, JSON · CC BY 4.0 Kosher-Certified Independent US Food Makers58 records · CSV, JSON · CC BY 4.0

Why the certifier columns matter

Almost any directory can tell you a product says halal or kosher on the label. Very few record who certified it, and without that the label cannot be checked. Those two tables are the part of this that does not exist anywhere else.

Corrections

If a record is wrong — a certifier changed, a maker closed, an owner sold — tell us and it gets fixed in the next version. Corrections are welcome from the makers themselves.
