Best Guinea Pig Food Worth the Hunt

Guinea pigs have one non-negotiable that separates their food from every other small pet's: they can't make their own vitamin C, so they need it delivered daily or they get scurvy. That means a vitamin-C-fortified pellet (not rabbit food, not a generic rodent mix), unlimited fresh grass hay as the real foundation, and vitamin-C-rich vegetables on top. Get those three right and a cavy thrives for years. Here are the makers that fortify properly and ship hay fresh.
