What to use instead of flour tortillas

It depends entirely on what it's doing in the dish. Here's the job, what else does it, and what to skip.

Advertisement

Large wrapper rolled around a wet filling

Wheat gluten plus the fat worked into the dough gives a sheet that stretches around a load and stays folded, and warmth is what makes it pliable.

Lavash or markook sheetsRan out
1 large sheet per 10-inch tortilla
Thinner and easier to roll tight, with no chew of its own. It dries into a cracker within minutes out of the bag, so fill it immediately.
Where to buy it →
Pocketless Greek pita or lafaRan out
1 per tortilla
Thicker and chewier, so it folds around a filling rather than rolling into a tube. Holds a much wetter filling without leaking.
Where to buy it →
Cassava flour tortillasGluten-free
Match one for one, warmed 30 seconds a side
Pliable while warm and brittle once cool, and they taste faintly sweet. They tear at the seam under a heavy burrito, so build them lighter.
Where to buy it →
Blanched collard or cabbage leavesGluten-free
2 large leaves per tortilla, blanched 45 seconds and dried
Wraps surprisingly tight and adds a green, brassica flavour. No starch to absorb juices, so the seam gets wet and it has to be eaten straight away.
What doesn't work: Corn tortillas. With no gluten there is nothing to stretch, so they crack across the fold as soon as the filling has any weight or moisture. They are right for a taco and wrong for anything rolled.

Warm base for a taco, fajita or quesadilla

It only has to fold once and take direct griddle heat, so pliability matters far less than flavour and how it handles a hot pan.

Corn tortillasGluten-free
2 small per large flour tortilla, 30 seconds a side on a dry comal
Far more corn flavour and a softer, more fragile fold. Doubling them up is standard for anything juicy.
Where to buy it →
Small pita roundsRan out
1 per tortilla, warmed 20 seconds a side
Thicker and breadier, so the filling ratio has to go up or the whole thing tastes of bread. Holds up to a fork.
Where to buy it →
Naan, halvedRan out
1 naan per 2 tortillas
Rich, chewy and much heavier. It browns hard in a dry pan because of the sugar in the dough, so keep it moving.
Where to buy it →
Butter lettuce or romaine leavesGluten-free
2 leaves per tortilla
Cold, crunchy and wet against a hot filling, and there is no starch to soak up the juices. It suits grilled meat far more than anything saucy.
What doesn't work: Hard taco shells for a saucy fajita filling. They shatter along the fold the moment they take on moisture and the filling ends up on the plate. A hard shell needs a dry filling and a soft one absorbs sauce.
