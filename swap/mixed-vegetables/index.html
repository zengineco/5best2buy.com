What to use instead of mixed vegetables

It depends entirely on what it's doing in the dish. Here's the job, what else does it, and what to skip.

Advertisement

Frozen diced veg stirred through a fried rice or stir-fry

They are blanched and diced before freezing, so they need two minutes and no knife work. The job is bulk, colour and a little sweetness rather than any one flavour.

Fresh carrot, peas and green beans, diced smallRan out
2 cups diced fresh per 2 cups frozen, cooked 3 to 4 min longer
Sweeter and firmer, and they release far less water, so a fried rice stays dry and the grains keep separate.
Where to buy it →
Frozen peas aloneRan out
1:1 by volume
One colour and one flavour, sweeter than the mix, and done in 60 seconds. Add them last so they stay bright green.
Cabbage, carrot and onion, shreddedRan out
1:1 by volume
Crunchier and more savoury. Cabbage gives off water, so keep the heat high and the pan uncrowded.
Frozen edamame and sweetcornRan out
1:1 by volume
Firmer bite and noticeably more protein. The corn sweetness is stronger than the standard mix.
Where to buy it →
What doesn't work: Tipping them into a hot wok straight from the bag. The ice on them drops the pan temperature and everything steams grey. Thaw and pat dry, or add them in the final minute with the heat as high as it goes.

The vegetable mass in a soup, stew or roasting tray

They are there for bulk, sweetness and body. Which vegetables they are matters far less than when each one goes into the pot.

Root vegetables, dicedRan out
1:1 by weight, in 15 min before anything softer
Sweeter and starchier, and the soup thickens on its own as they break down at the edges.
Where to buy it →
Winter squash, cubedRan out
1:1 by weight
Goes soft in about 20 minutes and starts collapsing, so the broth turns orange and thicker than you planned.
Where to buy it →
Cauliflower and broccoli floretsRan out
1:1 by weight, in for the last 10 min
Turns sulphurous over a long simmer, so add them late. They soak up more of the broth than root veg do.
Canned beans, drained and rinsedRan out
1:1 by volume, in for the last 5 min
Filling and creamy rather than fresh-tasting. They shed starch, so the pot goes cloudy and thicker.
Where to buy it →
What doesn't work: Salad vegetables such as cucumber, lettuce or radish to bulk out a soup. They are over 90 percent water, collapse into slime inside ten minutes and thin the broth on their way down.
