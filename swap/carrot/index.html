What to use instead of carrot

It depends entirely on what it's doing in the dish. Here's the job, what else does it, and what to skip.

Advertisement

Sweet aromatic in a mirepoix or soffritto

It is the sugar in the base. Cooked slowly it balances the onion's sharpness and the acid from wine or tomato, and it adds body as it breaks down.

ParsnipRan out
1:1 by weight, diced larger
Sweeter with an aniseed edge, and it collapses faster, which is why the dice goes up a size.
Where to buy it →
Red bell pepperRan out
1:1 by weight
Sweet with no starch, so the base stays looser and the colour goes orange-red.
Where to buy it →
Celeriac + sugarRan out
1:1 by weight, plus 1 tsp sugar per 2 carrots
Savoury and nutty. Without the sugar the finished stew tastes noticeably flat.
Where to buy it →
Sweet potatoRan out
1:1 by weight, added in the last 20 min
Much sweeter and it dissolves into the sauce, thickening it. Added early it disappears completely.
Where to buy it →
What doesn't work: Leaving the carrot out and adding a spoonful of sugar instead. Sugar sweetens, but a slowly softened carrot also brings body and a roasted-vegetable depth, so the sauce comes out sweet and thin.

Crunchy raw shred in a slaw or salad

Dense and low in water, so it holds its snap in dressing for hours where softer vegetables go limp within minutes.

Kohlrabi, juliennedRan out
1:1 by weight
Crisper and milder, and the closest match for texture. No orange colour in the bowl.
Where to buy it →
Daikon or watermelon radishRan out
1:1 by weight, salted and drained 20 min first
Peppery and much juicier. Skip the draining and it waters the dressing down within the hour.
Where to buy it →
JicamaRan out
1:1 by weight
Wetter and sweeter, and it snaps where carrot shreds. It holds its crunch in dressing for the best part of a day.
Where to buy it →
Red cabbage, shredded fineRan out
1:1 by volume
More bitter, and it bleeds purple through the whole bowl within an hour.
Where to buy it →
What doesn't work: Pre-shredded bagged carrot for a slaw you are making ahead. It was cut days earlier and has dried at the edges, so it goes limp in dressing within an hour where fresh-shredded holds a day.

Bulk vegetable that survives an hour in a stew

Dense and low in water, so it holds its shape and stays as distinct pieces while softer vegetables dissolve into the sauce.

Turnip or swedeRan out
1:1 by weight
Peppery and less sweet, so the stew needs a pinch of sugar or a splash of cider to balance.
Where to buy it →
ParsnipRan out
1:1 by weight, added 15 min later than the carrot would go in
Sweeter and softer, and it will fall apart if you give it the full hour.
Where to buy it →
Winter squash, cubedRan out
1:1 by weight, added in the last 25 min
Sweet and it thickens the sauce as the edges break down. In from the start it is soup.
Where to buy it →
Waxy potatoesRan out
1:1 by weight
No sweetness at all, and they soak up salt from the broth, so taste before you season at the end.
Where to buy it →
What doesn't work: Frozen sliced carrots in at the start. They were blanched before freezing, so an hour in a stew turns them into orange mush. Stir them in for the last 10 minutes.
