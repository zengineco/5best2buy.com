What to use instead of tomato

It depends entirely on what it's doing in the dish. Here's the job, what else does it, and what to skip.

Advertisement

Raw and diced for freshness and acidity

Mostly water with acid and glutamate. In pico de gallo or a sandwich the job is the wet, sharp bite against everything richer around it.

Cherry or grape tomatoes, halvedRan out
1 cup per 1 medium tomato
Sweeter with firmer skin, and far less juice pooling in the bowl. Usually better than a pale winter beefsteak.
Where to buy it →
Tomatillo, diced rawRan out
1:1 by weight
Sharper and greener with a slight stickiness. The salsa reads as a verde.
Where to buy it →
Red bell pepper + lime juiceRan out
1 cup diced pepper + 1 tsp lime juice per 1 medium tomato
Crunchy and sweet with no juice at all, so the acid has to come from the lime.
Where to buy it →
Canned diced tomatoes, drained hardRan out
1 cup drained per 1 medium tomato
Soft and cooked-tasting with no bite. Drain them in a sieve for 10 minutes or the salsa floods.
Where to buy it →
What doesn't work: Sun-dried tomatoes chopped into a fresh salsa. Concentrated, chewy and sweet-sour from the oil, they sit on the tongue like raisins and nothing about them reads as fresh.

Cooked down as the acid and body of a sauce or stew

As the water leaves, the sugars and glutamate concentrate, and the remaining acid keeps a rich braise from tasting flat.

Canned whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by handRan out
One 14 oz can per 4 medium fresh tomatoes
Better than most fresh tomatoes for this. More reliable acid, deeper red, and no peeling.
Where to buy it →
Tomato paste + waterRan out
2 tbsp paste + 3/4 cup water per 2 medium tomatoes, plus 1 tsp vinegar
Sweeter, thicker and much darker. The vinegar goes back in because paste has lost its brightness.
Where to buy it →
PassataRan out
3/4 cup per 2 medium tomatoes
Smooth with no pulp, so the sauce has no texture. It reduces faster, so watch it.
Where to buy it →
Roasted red peppers + red wine vinegarRan out
1 cup peppers + 1 tbsp vinegar per 2 medium tomatoes
Sweet and smoky with no tomato tang, and the sauce comes out orange. Useful in February.
Where to buy it →
What doesn't work: Ketchup as the tomato in a savoury braise. It is roughly a quarter sugar with vinegar and clove already in it, so the stew ends up sweet, sharp and tasting of the bottle.
